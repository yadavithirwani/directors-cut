SCENE 1 - INT. APARTMENT - DAY

SARAH (30s, sharp eyes, leather jacket) holds a metallic BRIEFCASE tight against her chest. 
JACK (40s, weary detective) stands near the window, staring out.

JACK
If you step out that door with the briefcase, there's no coming back.

SARAH
It's not about coming back, Jack. It's about finishing what we started.

Sarah places the BRIEFCASE on the wooden dining table. 

SCENE 2 - EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT

Rain pours over the neon-lit tarmac. Sarah walks briskly under an umbrella. 
She glides past a BLACK SEDAN parked by the curb.

SCENE 3 - INT. WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

A cavernous industrial warehouse filled with shipping crates. 
MARCUS (50s, menacing suit) waits in the shadows.

SARAH enters. She is holding the metallic BRIEFCASE.

MARCUS
You brought it. Good. Open it up.

SARAH
Only after I see the security override code.

Sarah sets the BRIEFCASE on a steel container.
